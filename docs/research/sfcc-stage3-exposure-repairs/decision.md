cylinder/twisted-side family under the ordered binary ancestor class
`roundMax(max(chamferMax(max(roundMin(L,E),C),-G),-K),S)`.

The proof reuses the correlated source/profile/lathe bounds, checks every ancestor
inactive, retains a nonzero transverse determinant and separately bounds the
numerical point against the ideal branch. It binds the actual runtime tree by a

preserves its mathematical expressions; `ConsumedPiece` retains the feature-set
reference needed for own-curve queries. Unsupported structures/transforms return

## Why the endpoint handling needs more than blanket unwiring

A/B correctly block an unknown bound from acquiring arbitrary corner authority
through candidate creation, nearest snapping, parameter replacement, coordinate
override and incidence wiring. Original-end flags survive splits and wrap merges.
However, the experiments demonstrate that withdrawing those operations globally
also withdraws genuine modeled junction incidence. A global unresolved count is
insufficient, but globally refusing all uncertain junction handling is likewise
not a complete reconstruction repair.

Source 31 is the generated **compound chamfer diagonal** from operand fields,
not simply the whole chamfer-max field. The missing `[10,31]` membership must not
be dismissed as a phantom from its numeric IDs. Where the inner union is inactive,
source 30 can coincide with native source 10, so `[10,31]` and `[30,31]` may describe
an equivalent local physical attachment. But B does not establish or transport
that equivalence. Removing or rebinding a required label needs actual immutable
source/domain/branch and parameter-interval evidence; proximity or the surviving
`[30,31]` label alone is insufficient. The preservation rejection stands.

### Concrete next revision: resolve a bounded frontier, not a boolean

The next repair round should keep the proven positive exposure work and add the
smallest source-justified endpoint-resolution mechanism:

1. **Retain bounded unresolved frontier data.** For each affected run end keep
   the original source/branch and raw parameter frame, orientation, last known
   positive event, unresolved bound/interval and why classification stopped
   (numerical resolution, conditioning, work limit or unsupported source). Do not
   collapse the information needed for a later connection into only a boolean.
   Keep this per-end and bounded; a general trace-history framework is unnecessary.
2. **Distinguish a real junction from an unresolved search cutoff.** A later
   resolver may recover endpoint authority only from actual source evidence for
   the junction and connection along the same branch inside the retained frontier.
   A nearby existing corner, three approximately zero fields, a successful Newton
   return or a large incident-curve count is not enough. Coincident source aliases
   can inflate that count without independent equations.
3. **Alternatively certify the same physical arc attachment.** If a retained
   source-labelled arc already reaches a justified junction, prove equality or
   transport for the corresponding immutable source occurrences, domains, fixed
   branch and ordered parameter interval. Then retain all required source labels
   on the physical connection. A coordinate match, similar tangent or surviving
   `[30,31]` label alone cannot authorize the missing `[10,31]` attachment. The
   retained endpoint itself also needs justified source validity; equivalence
   must not transfer an unproved endpoint into authority.
4. **Preserve unresolved behavior on failure.** A resolver that exhausts its
   bound or cannot prove the connection leaves the end unresolved/unwired and
   reports that status. It must not retry unconditional legacy nearest snapping,
   force unknown to alive, widen the merge radius or silently restore a duplicate
   edge. Handle A's bisection iteration limit explicitly as unresolved.
5. **Require both exits in one fresh actual compilation.** The target physical
   and alias representations must cover the positive source interval with correct
   source roles/order, and the genuine original lower junction must retain its
   required incidences. Keep the opposite-profile/source-selection control, true
   terminal/hidden/capped/distinct-source controls, near-existing-corner rejection,
   budget exhaustion and lossless uncertainty propagation through split/wrap/remake.
   Write receipts before any assertion so a new collateral failure remains visible.

This contract does not pre-authorize a particular junction theorem or treat the
old lower-junction labels as infallible. If a label is challenged, the revision
must prove that challenge from the full source and preserve every genuine geometry
and metadata obligation. It cannot make the gate disappear by reinterpreting an
