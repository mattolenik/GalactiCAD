## Review corrections

The initial `cylinderGraphDerivativeLower` used ordinary division after interval arithmetic. That could round a claimed lower bound upward by an ULP. Root's Reviewed variant replaces it with interval division and `.lo`. The quantity is the magnitude of the carrier's X partial derivative, `|radialX|/radius`; it is not dX/dZ or an approximation-error bound.

I also found a missing explicit twist-clamp premise. The helper computed the unclamped twist angle, while the actual extrusion clamps `(y-posY+h)/(2h)`. Native-side dominance over the cap alone does not force the height inside the unclamped interval when E is positive. The actual first fan is safely inside, and the E<=0 positive-exposure branch already implies that restriction, but the general counterexample branch needs an explicit guard. The final Clamp source adds a finite, positive-height, outward interval height-in-range restriction and direct one-ULP-outside/nonfinite controls. This prevents an off-fixture unsupported twist branch from acquiring proof authority without introducing generalized clamp handling.

The selected source includes both corrections. Neither changes mesh options, the existing geometric source, tolerances or numerical query limits.
