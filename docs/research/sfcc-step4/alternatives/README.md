# Independent alternatives retained for consensus

All three competitors started from accepted Step 3 revision
`f804044d8c960acea3b3f8735678eecffa31952d`, worked in isolated copies, finished
before the distinct fourth reviewer began, and did not inspect one another's
solutions. The fourth reviewed their connected source, not only vote counts or
aggregate test results. Production integrates B only.

These directories retain the original final handoffs, source/artifact manifests,
reports/proofs, reviewed runtime patches and raw preliminary timings. They are
**historical competitor evidence**. Their original temporary paths and reserved
consensus/coordinator checks remain as recorded; they are not statements that
those checks are still pending in the final consensus. Original complete patches
had SHA-256:

- A: `f9bfd1160c919467cfba05bba0108e9f40b8d77165b73c5c42e4c59164d37908`.
- B: `e640e904f6f8003fa961cea692f6da3db58b96db1d03ebd5ca4c9037037ec856`.
- C: `62e8a7d42bd7ff10ffa611423b9ada28bc3fc0a736efac2da3aaa6b7f037395a`.

The smaller `runtime.patch` files here intentionally omit each competitor's
redundant harness, copied logs and generated artifacts. A's report points to
some evidence retained only in its original isolated copy; its retained source
manifest and handoff identify that copy. Current consensus evidence and runnable
common harness live one directory above and in the kernel examples.

A's immutable borrowed preparation has a credible lifetime and arithmetic
argument, but its preliminary gains were modest relative to six touched runtime
files, new context plumbing and O(terminal scopes) retained entries. C's lazy pin
index has a sound exact conflict-fold argument and exclusive-borrow source
lifetime, but its large-box improvement coincided with a repeatable unexplained
sphere regression. Both are rejected for this milestone, not declared
mathematically incorrect. Their gains must not be added to B's measurements.

B's two constant-size call-local successful-result memos address the strongest
measured duplicate-operand cost without extending a lifetime or introducing
allocation. The consensus measurement gate still evaluates its ordinary cases;
source simplicity alone is not performance evidence.

B's original 13-case `wasm-comparison.json` was reproduced byte-for-byte by
the independent final consensus. It is stored once at
[the final comparison](../wasm-comparison.json);
`b/reused-evidence.json` records its original hash and size.
