## Why the endpoint handling needs more than blanket unwiring

A/B correctly block an unknown bound from acquiring arbitrary corner authority
through candidate creation, nearest snapping, parameter replacement, coordinate
override and incidence wiring. Original-end flags survive splits and wrap merges.
However, the experiments demonstrate that withdrawing those operations globally
also withdraws genuine modeled junction incidence. A global unresolved count is
insufficient, but globally refusing all uncertain junction handling is likewise
not a complete reconstruction repair.

Source 31 is the generated **compound chamfer diagonal** from operand fields,
not simply the whole chamfer-max field. The missing `[10,31]` membership must not
be dismissed as a phantom from its numeric IDs. Where the inner union is inactive,
source 30 can coincide with native source 10, so `[10,31]` and `[30,31]` may describe
an equivalent local physical attachment. But B does not establish or transport
that equivalence. Removing or rebinding a required label needs actual immutable
source/domain/branch and parameter-interval evidence; proximity or the surviving
`[30,31]` label alone is insufficient. The preservation rejection stands.
