The global step in the result's short theorem is valid under those checked hypotheses, with the following explicit argument. Fix any interpolation time s. All image triangles have the original positive relative orientation; internal oriented edge contributions cancel. For any point outside the finite image edge set, the sum of triangle winding numbers therefore equals the winding number of the unchanged simple outer boundary: one in the disk and zero outside. Every containing image triangle contributes positively, so a generic interior point has exactly one preimage and an exterior point has none. This proves no open overlap or gap.

At an interior edge, the adjacent triangles lie on opposite sides of its image and cover a neighborhood. At an interior vertex, successive nonzero image triangles give positive angular sectors; the closed fan has positive integer winding, hence covers a neighborhood of its image. Thus the PL map is open on the disk interior, including its skeleton. An interior point mapping onto the outer boundary would create exterior images, contrary to zero exterior multiplicity. Two distinct interior preimages of the same image would create a generic nearby double preimage, contrary to multiplicity one. The fixed simple boundary is itself injective. The continuous map is consequently a bijection from a compact disk onto itself and hence a homeomorphism. This works for each s, so every straight image path stays in the disk.

Along such a path, the continuous old mesh graph has derivative bounded by the maximum graph Jacobian over all original triangles. Its change is bounded by L_mesh times beta even when the disk is nonconvex: the path remains in the disk because every intermediate map does. The source displacement and unchanged same-barycentric f32 error then give delta + L_mesh*beta + eta. No global convexity assumption is needed.

This remains a conditional API. It accepts a JSON source receipt whose source/triangle identity is not internally cryptographically linked to the Domain, and it does not prove its target roots.
