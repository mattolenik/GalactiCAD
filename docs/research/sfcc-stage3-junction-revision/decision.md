The remaining ancestors are checked inactive at the enclosed root. Nonzero axial
offset derivative isolates its intersection with that helix.

`0.8320502943378428`, and the other-edge distance margin is approximately 1 mm.
These are source-location and numerical-association facts, not complete junction

chamfer field. Its piecewise behavior matters; the location theorem must not be
read as enumerating all incident physical arcs. Generated compound 31 stores
rounded weights and offset. The ideal source radius and `offset/weight` must remain
distinct until their association is bounded. A correctly makes no exact equality
claim between those two equations and performs no attachment using this theorem.
